# Vehicles

The **SackReligious drop van** is the story vehicle: black base, gold branding, deep green accents. Used for pickup / deliveries.

Traffic rules (world-life + court integrity):

- Stay on lanes, face travel direction
- Follow distance, brake, queue
- Avoid the court AABB, buildings, and Benji
- No sideways sliding or sidewalk cutting as the intended path

Do not skin the whole city in brand wraps.
