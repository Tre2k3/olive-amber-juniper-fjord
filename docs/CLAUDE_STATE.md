## Done 2026-10-23 (pass 6 — bowling)
- `play/bowling.ts` (pure): the roll is simulated, never decided. A/D sets the stance/line; holding Space runs a four-step approach while the swing loads (the meter is the swing); releasing at the bottom of the swing (approach 0.68–0.88) gives a clean ball with revs; early = lofted slow ball; past the foul line = foul (zero, like the real rule). Ball physics: lane friction, oil/dry zones, a right-hander's hook (~0.3 m drift on full revs), gutters at ±0.53 m. Pins: the real ten-pin rack, circle contact, ~10° ball deflection so a pocket ball weaves 1-3-5-9, and a **directional** chain reaction — a pin flies along the line it was struck, so the pocket strikes (~27%) and a flat head-on ball leaves splits (strikes ~17%). Deterministic per roll via seed.
