# World

Memphis is a continuous 64×48 tile city (Beale, Union, Poplar, 3rd, Front, Highland).

## Districts

- West Side — Benji's apartment
- Midtown — SackReligious HQ, RCM WORX luxury lot (west of HQ on Union)
- South Memphis — 901 / Sackrow court, 901 Lanes bowling alley east of the court
- East Memphis — neighborhood drop
- Downtown
- South Main — culture spot
- Union Ave — drop van
- Beale, Harbor (Pyramid), Riverfront

## Life

Pedestrians walk sidewalks. Traffic stays on lanes from `worldTopology` with stop / follow distance. Integrity passes punch the court out of the road, keep furniture solid, and keep HQ / apartment doorways walkable.

Interiors: apartment and HQ are dedicated spaces with wall-shell collision and a real doorway. K Blanco talks inside HQ. 901 Lanes is the pink-neon bowling alley east of 901 Court. RCM WORX is a Midtown lot with the black Mercedes-Benz Sprinter and Cadillac Escalade ESV — book a VIP drop.

## Objective markers

HUD compass + minimap point at the active step. Only the current objective dominates.

Playtest Kit (KIT / F3) is an in-game debug overlay: live coords + FPS, warp to landmarks, cheats, and a copyable bug dump for screenshots.
