# Game design

**Fantasy:** Move through Memphis, help K Blanco prepare the SackReligious drop, build Respect, earn $ackdollars, wear the brand, prove it at the 901 Court, then watch the drop go live.

**Loop:** Explore → meet people → complete objectives → earn Respect and $ackdollars → unlock clothing / opportunities → Drop Live → After Hours → keep roaming. **901 Strip** is a 3-lap street race vs Cam (CPU) on Beale / Highland / Poplar / 3rd / Union / Front. Beat him for $140, 10 Respect, and the Strip King trophy.

## Currencies

- **$ackdollars** — virtual outfits and in-game buys
- **Respect** — reputation. Unlocks experiences (Moneybag Chain at 20). Earned from missions, help, basketball, culture.

## Not this game

Not GTA. Not a walking sim. Not a Three.js demo. Not an ad banner with a joystick.

## Art lock

Stylized illustrated / cel-shaded 2.5D. Approved Benji walk cycle. Equipped apparel **recolors Benji's garment** and stamps the matching product graphic (not a ground ring). Approved K Blanco (1.22 × 2.52 vs Benji 1.15 × 1.85). Sackrow court as the north star. Drop van branded; civilian cars ordinary.
